# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Project participation should be respectful, constructive, and welcoming. Examples
include giving actionable feedback, respecting different experiences, and focusing
discussion on the software and its documented behavior.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, sustained
disruption, and publishing another person's private information are unacceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other spaces
where someone represents this project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when behavior violates this policy. For a private conduct
report, contact the repository owner through GitHub or open a private security
advisory with `[Conduct]` in its title. Reports will be reviewed confidentially and
handled proportionately and consistently.
